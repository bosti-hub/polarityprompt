# A good cup of coffee

[Click Start listening, then read this text aloud.]

## The beans

Every good cup starts with fresh beans. Coffee beans are the seeds of a red fruit that grows on
small trees in warm countries. After the harvest the seeds are dried, sorted and shipped all over
the world.

Roasting turns the green seeds brown and creates the flavour. A light roast tastes fruity and
bright, while a dark roast tastes bitter, heavy and a little smoky. Buy whole beans when you can,
and grind them just before brewing.

## Brewing

Water matters more than most people think. Use clean water just below boiling. Water that is too
hot burns the coffee, and water that is too cool leaves it weak and sour.

For a filter, use roughly one spoon of ground coffee for every cup. Pour a little water first and
let the grounds bloom for half a minute. Then pour the rest slowly in circles, and be patient
while it drips through.

## Enjoy

[Pause, look into the camera.] Pour the coffee into a warm mug and take a moment before the first
sip. Smell the aroma, notice the colour, and taste it slowly. Milk and sugar are welcome, but try
it black at least once.
